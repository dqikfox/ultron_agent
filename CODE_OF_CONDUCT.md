# ULTRON Agent Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

### ✅ Positive Behaviors

* **Demonstrating empathy and kindness** toward other people
* **Being respectful** of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
* **Giving and gracefully accepting** constructive feedback
* **Accepting responsibility** and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
* **Focusing on what is best** not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community
* **Using welcoming and inclusive language**
* **Being patient with newcomers** and helping them learn
* **Celebrating diverse perspectives** and creative solutions
* **Acknowledging contributions** from all community members

### ❌ Unacceptable Behaviors

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* **Harassment and discrimination** of any kind
* **The use of sexualized language or imagery**, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
* **Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments**, and personal or political attacks
* **Public or private harassment**
* **Publishing others' private information**, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
* **Spam or off-topic content** that disrupts community discussions
* **Deliberately intimidating, stalking, or following**
* **Advocating for, or encouraging, any of the above behavior**
* **Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate** in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces. Examples of representing our community include:

* Using an official e-mail address
* Posting via an official social media account
* Acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event
* Participating in project-related forums, mailing lists, or chat platforms
* Contributing to project repositories or related discussions

## Community Guidelines

### For Contributors

* **Be collaborative**: Work together to achieve common goals
* **Be constructive**: Focus on solutions and improvements
* **Be inclusive**: Welcome people of all backgrounds and skill levels
* **Be patient**: Understand that people have different learning styles and speeds
* **Be professional**: Maintain a respectful tone in all communications

### For Maintainers

* **Be responsive**: Address issues and questions in a timely manner
* **Be fair**: Apply standards consistently across all contributors
* **Be transparent**: Explain decisions and processes clearly
* **Be supportive**: Help contributors succeed and grow
* **Be decisive**: Make clear decisions when consensus isn't reached

### For All Community Members

* **Assume good intentions**: Give others the benefit of the doubt
* **Ask questions**: Seek clarification when something is unclear
* **Provide context**: Help others understand your perspective
* **Stay on topic**: Keep discussions relevant to the project
* **Use appropriate channels**: Post in the right place for your message type

## Reporting Guidelines

### How to Report

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by:

1. **For non-urgent issues**: Create a private issue or discussion
2. **For urgent issues**: Contact the project maintainers directly
3. **For sensitive issues**: Use the GitHub Security Advisory feature
4. **Anonymous reporting**: Use community reporting tools if available

### What to Include

When reporting, please include:

* **Description** of the incident or behavior
* **Context** surrounding the situation
* **Evidence** (screenshots, links, etc.) if available
* **Impact** on you or the community
* **Desired outcome** or resolution

### Response Process

All reports will be:

* **Acknowledged** within 48 hours
* **Investigated** promptly and thoroughly
* **Handled confidentially** with respect for all parties
* **Resolved** with appropriate action taken
* **Followed up** to ensure satisfaction

## Enforcement

### Investigation Process

When a report is received:

1. **Initial Review**: Community leaders assess the report
2. **Investigation**: Gather additional information if needed
3. **Deliberation**: Determine appropriate response
4. **Action**: Implement consequences if warranted
5. **Follow-up**: Monitor for resolution and prevent recurrence

### Consequences

Community leaders will determine consequences based on the severity and nature of the violation:

#### 1. Correction
**Community Impact**: Minor violation or misunderstanding.
**Consequence**: Private written warning with clarification of expectations and a request for behavior modification.

#### 2. Warning
**Community Impact**: Single incident or pattern of inappropriate behavior.
**Consequence**: Formal warning with specified consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with involved parties for a specified time period.

#### 3. Temporary Ban
**Community Impact**: Serious violation or sustained inappropriate behavior.
**Consequence**: Temporary ban from community interactions and spaces for a specified period. No public or private interaction with involved parties during this time.

#### 4. Permanent Ban
**Community Impact**: Severe violation, sustained harassment, or aggression toward community members.
**Consequence**: Permanent ban from all community interactions and spaces.

### Appeals Process

If you believe you have been unfairly sanctioned:

1. **Submit an appeal** within 30 days of the decision
2. **Provide additional context** or evidence
3. **Request review** by different community leaders
4. **Accept the final decision** of the appeals process
